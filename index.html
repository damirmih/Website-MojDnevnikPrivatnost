Politika privatnosti

Moj Dnevnik je dnevnik ocena, raspored časova i planer obaveza za srednjoškolce. Sve što u njega upišeš ostaje kod tebe.

Ukratko

Aplikacija ne prikuplja nikakve podatke o tebi. Nema naloga ni registracije, nema reklama, nema analitike, nema praćenja i ne postoji server na koji bi se tvoji podaci slali. Sve što uneseš čuva se na tvom telefonu, a ako uključiš iCloud (iPhone) ili Google rezervnu kopiju (Android), i u tvom ličnom nalogu.

01Ko stoji iza aplikacije

Aplikaciju razvija Damir Mihajlović kao pojedinac, ne kao firma. Nema zaposlenih, saradnika ni partnera koji bi imali pristup bilo čemu što je u aplikaciji.

03Gde se ti podaci nalaze

U bazi unutar same aplikacije, na tvom uređaju. Ta baza je zaštićena kao i svi ostali podaci aplikacija na iPhone-u i druge aplikacije joj ne mogu pristupiti.

Deo tih podataka aplikacija upisuje i u zajednički prostor sa svojim widgetima, isključivo da bi widget na početnom i zaključanom ekranu mogao da prikaže tvoj sledeći čas, prosek ili tajmer učenja. Taj prostor ne napušta uređaj.

04iCloud sinhronizacija

Ako na uređaju imaš uključen iCloud Drive, aplikacija koristi tvoj lični iCloud nalog da bi isti podaci bili dostupni na svim tvojim Apple uređajima. Ti podaci putuju između tvojih uređaja i Apple-ovog iCloud servisa, i njih čuva Apple pod Apple-ovom politikom privatnosti.

Autor aplikacije nema pristup toj kopiji. To je privatni deo tvog iCloud naloga, isto kao i podaci bilo koje druge aplikacije koja koristi iCloud.

Ako ne želiš sinhronizaciju, isključi iCloud Drive za ovu aplikaciju u Podešavanjima telefona. Aplikacija tada radi potpuno lokalno.

05Obaveštenja

Aplikacija te može podsetiti na kontrolni ili obavezu, a iz rasporeda i na to da je ostalo još nekoliko minuta do kraja časa. Ta obaveštenja zakazuje sam telefon i ona nikada ne izlaze sa uređaja — ne postoji server koji ih šalje. Dozvolu za obaveštenja tražimo kada prvi put postaviš podsetnik. Na iPhone-u je tražimo i pri pokretanju aplikacije čim tvoj raspored ima bar jedan čas, jer su obaveštenja pred kraj časa podrazumevano uključena (možeš ih isključiti u podešavanjima rasporeda). Na Androidu se dozvola ne traži pri pokretanju, pa obaveštenja pred kraj časa stižu tek pošto odobriš dozvolu. Dozvolu možeš uskratiti; sve ostalo u aplikaciji radi i bez nje.

06Kopija podataka i deljenje rasporeda

U Podešavanjima možeš da napraviš kopiju svojih podataka kao link ili QR kod, i da je uvezeš na drugom telefonu. Kopiju pravi aplikacija na tvoj zahtev, a ti sam biraš koje kategorije ulaze u nju i kome je šalješ. Aplikacija je nikuda ne šalje sama. Pošto link i QR kod sadrže izabrane podatke (na primer ocene i beleške), šalji ih samo sebi ili nekome kome veruješ. Podaci stoje u delu linka iza znaka #, koji se ne šalje nijednom serveru, a stranica na koju link vodi čita ih samo u tvom pregledaču.

Raspored časova možeš da pošalješ i kao sliku, link ili QR kod. Link i QR kod rasporeda u sebi nose samo raspored (nazive predmeta, učionice i vreme časova), bez ocena i bez nastavnika. Aplikacija link ne šalje sama, ti biraš kome ga daješ. Stranica ne koristi kolačiće, analitiku ni spoljne fontove, ali se nalazi na GitHub Pages, pa GitHub, kao i svaki veb-server, može da vidi IP adresu onoga ko je otvori. Dugme za preuzimanje aplikacije vodi u prodavnicu sa oznakom izvora („raspored“), preko koje Apple odnosno Google autoru pokazuju samo zbirne brojke preuzimanja, ne podatke o tebi.

07Veza ka elektronskom dnevniku

Aplikacija ima prečicu koja u pregledaču otvara adresu elektronskog dnevnika. Podrazumevana adresa je esdnevnik.rs, a u Podešavanjima je možeš promeniti na adresu dnevnika svoje škole. Kada je otvoriš, nalaziš se na tom sajtu, na koji ova politika više ne važi — tamo važe pravila te škole odnosno servisa. Aplikacija ne čita ništa sa te stranice i ne zna se ni da li si se na nju prijavio.

Aplikacija nije povezana sa Ministarstvom prosvete ni sa esDnevnikom.

08Android

Na Androidu aplikacija radi na isti način: bez naloga, reklama i analitike, a podaci ostaju na telefonu. Umesto iCloud-a, Android može da napravi automatsku rezervnu kopiju podataka aplikacije (ocene, raspored, obaveze, beleške i podešavanja) u tvom ličnom Google nalogu (Google Auto Backup), ako je rezervna kopija uključena u Podešavanjima telefona. Tu kopiju čuva Google pod Google-ovom politikom privatnosti, autor aplikacije nema pristup njoj, a možeš je isključiti u Podešavanjima telefona (obično pod Google, Rezervna kopija). Prečica ka elektronskom dnevniku otvara adresu u pregledaču na telefonu, pa tamo važe pravila tog sajta, kao što je opisano u odeljku 07.

11Brisanje podataka

Kada obrišeš aplikaciju sa telefona, baza se briše zajedno sa njom. Ako si koristio iCloud, ta kopija se briše kroz Podešavanja telefona, u odeljku iCloud, gde možeš ukloniti podatke ove aplikacije.

Pošto nema servera, ne postoji nijedan primerak tvojih podataka koji bi trebalo tražiti da se obriše negde drugde.

ENPrivacy Policy

Moj Dnevnik (“My Grade Diary”) is a grade tracker, timetable and homework planner for high‑school students. Everything you put into it stays with you.

In short

The app collects no personal data whatsoever. There is no account or sign‑up, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no server for your data to be sent to. Everything you enter is stored on your own phone, and — if you enable it — in your own iCloud account (iPhone) or Google backup (Android).

Who makes it

The app is developed by Damir Mihajlović as an individual, not a company. Nobody else has access to anything inside the app.

What is stored

Only what you type in yourself: subjects, grades (1–5, plus/minus marks, conduct and descriptive marks), your timetable, assignments and study‑timer durations, notes, reminders and app settings. The app never asks for your name, email, phone number, school, location, contacts or photos, and requests no system permissions beyond notifications.

Where it lives

In a database inside the app on your device, protected by iOS like any other app’s data. A small part of it is also written to a shared container so the app’s own widgets can show your next class, your average or a running study timer. That container never leaves the device.

iCloud

If iCloud Drive is on, the app uses your personal iCloud account so the same data is available on all of your Apple devices. That copy is held by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. The developer cannot access it. Turning iCloud Drive off for this app makes it fully local.

Notifications

Reminders for tests and assignments, and “minutes left until the end of class” alerts from your timetable, are scheduled locally by your phone and never leave the device — no server sends them. Permission is requested when you set your first reminder. On iPhone it is also requested at launch as soon as your timetable has at least one lesson, because the end-of-class alerts are on by default (you can turn them off in the timetable settings). On Android it is not requested at launch, so end-of-class alerts only arrive once you grant the permission. You can decline; everything else works without it.

Backups

In Settings you can create a backup of your data as a link or a QR code and import it on another phone. The app creates it at your request; you choose which categories go in and who receives it. The app never sends it anywhere on its own. Because the link and QR code contain the selected data (for example grades and notes), send them only to yourself or someone you trust. The data sits in the part of the link after the # sign, which is not sent to any server, and the page the link opens reads it only in your browser.

Sharing your schedule

You can also share your timetable as an image, a link or a QR code. The schedule link and QR code carry only the schedule itself (subject names, classrooms and lesson times), with no grades and no teachers. The app never sends the link on its own; you choose who gets it. The page uses no cookies, analytics or external fonts, but it is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub, like any web server, can see the IP address of whoever opens it. The download button leads to the app store with a source tag (“raspored”), through which Apple or Google show the developer only aggregate download counts, never data about you.

Link to your school’s e‑diary

The app can open an electronic diary in the browser. The default address is esdnevnik.rs, and you can change it in Settings to your own school’s diary. Once there, that site’s own privacy terms apply, not this policy. The app reads nothing from that page. The app is not affiliated with the Ministry of Education or with esDnevnik.

Android

On Android the app works the same way: no accounts, ads or analytics, and your data stays on the phone. Instead of iCloud, Android may make an automatic backup of the app’s data (grades, timetable, assignments, notes and settings) to your personal Google account (Google Auto Backup), if backup is turned on in your phone’s Settings. That copy is held by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy, the developer cannot access it, and you can turn it off in your phone’s Settings (usually under Google, Backup). The shortcut to the electronic diary opens the address in the phone’s browser, so that site’s own terms apply, as described above.

What the app never does

No ads. No analytics. No cross‑app or cross‑site tracking. No third‑party SDKs or libraries of any kind. No accounts or passwords. No selling or sharing of data — there is none to sell.

Children

The app is made for students and is rated suitable for all ages. Because it collects nothing and shows no advertising, there is nothing that could be gathered about a child of any age.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its database with it. If you used iCloud, that copy can be removed from your iPhone’s Settings under iCloud. As there is no server, no copy of your data exists anywhere else.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change. This version is effective 8 October 2026.
